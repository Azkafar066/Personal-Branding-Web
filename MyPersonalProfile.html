My Personal Introduction

My name is Azka Farid Arsyad, and you can call me Azka. I am a student who enjoys helping others in doing good, exploring creativity through design, playing basketball, and watching football matches.

I believe that life is not only about achieving personal success, but also about becoming someone who brings positive contributions to others.

I aspire to become an Ustadz and an entrepreneur. I want to share beneficial Islamic knowledge and contribute positively to society through da'wah. At the same time, I hope to build a successful business that allows me to support my parents, help people in need, and bring benefits to others.
